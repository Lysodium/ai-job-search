# AI Job Search for Claude Cowork

Your job search, run by Claude: build a profile once, then find matching jobs, get a tailored CV and cover letter for each one, have application forms filled in for you (you always press Submit yourself), prepare for interviews, and track everything.

## Get started

1. Install this plugin (click **Install** on the plugin card).
2. In a new Cowork task, say **"Set up my job search."**
3. Claude asks where to keep your job-search folder (for example *Documents*), creates it, and walks you through your profile. Having your CV or LinkedIn PDF handy makes this faster.

## Things you can say

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Find me new jobs" | Searches job boards for postings that match your profile |
| "Rank my jobs" | Scores what was found and gives you a shortlist |
| "Apply to this job: <link>" | Checks the fit, then writes a tailored CV + cover letter (PDF) |
| "Fill out the application for Acme" | Fills the online form in your browser and stops before Submit |
| "Prepare me for my interview at Acme" | Builds a prep pack and can run a mock interview |
| "I got an interview with Acme" / "Acme rejected me" | Updates your tracker |
| "Check my email for application updates" | Reads Gmail (if connected) and proposes tracker updates |
| "Show my job dashboard" | Opens your application tracker |
| "What should I learn next?" | Finds skill gaps across your target jobs |

## Good to know

- Everything stays in your job-search folder on your computer. Your CVs, letters and tracker are never uploaded anywhere unless you ask.
- Claude never submits an application, solves a CAPTCHA, or answers legal/demographic questions for you.
- Gmail and Notion features need those connectors turned on in Cowork.
